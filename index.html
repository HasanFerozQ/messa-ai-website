FAQ

Questions clients ask before they buy.

How long does it take?

Most chatbots: 7 days. Most websites: 7–14 days. Larger stores or complex integrations can take longer, and we tell you upfront.

Do I need a website already for a chatbot?

No. If you have one, we train the bot on it. If you don't, the Bundle builds you a website with the bot built in.

What if the bot does not know an answer?

It says so honestly, captures the customer's name and question, and hands off to you. It never invents prices, availability, or policies.

Are WhatsApp costs included?

No, and we show them upfront. WhatsApp Business and platform fees depend on volume and provider, and are paid by you separately.

Who owns the website and bot?

You do. You get the site, the bot setup, and your content. We just build and, if you choose care, maintain it.

What about GDPR / UK GDPR?

We collect only what the bot needs to answer or book, we link a clear privacy note, and we never sell customer data. For regulated businesses, we add a human review step.
